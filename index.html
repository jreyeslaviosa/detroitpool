Water

Fresh water at 1,000 kg/m³ (62.4 lb/ft³). Floor load is the weight spread evenly over each pool's footprint, so it assumes a continuous base. Legs or runners concentrate the load and need their own check.

Reference loads

IBC Table 1607.1 (Michigan's code is based on the IBC): residential 40 psf, assembly with fixed seats 60 psf, open assembly and lobbies 100 psf. These are requirements for new buildings, not the rating of this floor. A 1940s timber floor may have been designed to a lower standard or weakened over time.

Wall push

The outward force from the water per length of wall. It grows with the square of the depth and acts a third of the way up from the bottom.
